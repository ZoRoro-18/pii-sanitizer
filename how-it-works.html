Architecture & Detection Engine

A transparent look at RedactLocal's single-pass pipeline, checksum validation, priority conflict resolution, and live self-tests.

Open Sanitizer

6-Stage Processing Pipeline

Detectors run independently against unmodified text so earlier matches never mutate offsets for later patterns.

01 · INPUT

Text or CSV Parse

Plain text or RFC 4180 cell-by-cell split preserving quotes & line endings.

02 · DETECTION

6 Detectors

Parallel scans collect candidate spans with boundary & context guards.

03 · VALIDATION

Checksums

Luhn (cards), Verhoeff (Aadhaar), octet bounds (IPv4), and IPv6 syntax.

04 · OVERLAP

Priority Resolve

Filters Luhn-rejected zones and resolves overlaps greedily by rank & length.

05 · REDACTION

Transform

Applies Full Mask, Partial Mask, or Consistent Pseudonymization map.

06 · OUTPUT

XSS-Safe Export

Escapes HTML entities for DOM diff and builds complete Copy/Download output.

Detector Rules & Priority Order

Conflict resolution strictly follows: Credit Card (1) > Email (2) > IPv6 (3) > IPv4 (4) > Aadhaar (5) > PAN (6) > Phone (7).

Credit Card Priority 1

13–19 digits with optional space/hyphen separators. Validated via Luhn checksum; brands Visa, Mastercard, Amex, RuPay, or Card. Failed candidates create exclusion zones.

Valid: 4532-0151-1283-0366 Rejected: 4532-0151-1283-0367
Email Priority 2

RFC-practical local/domain pattern with malformed dot guards. Normalized to trimmed lowercase so case variants share pseudonyms.

Valid: PRIYA.SHARMA@finserve.in Key: priya.sharma@finserve.in
IPv4 & IPv6 Priority 3–4

IPv4 enforces strict 0–255 octets and rejects software versions (v1.2.3.4) or 5-octet strings. IPv6 validates full 8-group & :: compressed syntax.

Valid: 192.168.1.45, fe80::1 Rejected: 999.256.1.1, 14:35:09
Indian Aadhaar Priority 5

12 digits starting with 2–9 in uniform 4-4-4 spacing/hyphens or contiguous form. Validated by the Verhoeff dihedral checksum when strict check is ON.

Valid: 4829 3104 7561 Rejected: 4829 3104 7562
Indian PAN Priority 6

Strict uppercase 10-character identifier matching [A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z] with word boundaries.

Valid: ABCDE1234F Rejected: abcde1234f, ABCDE1234F1
Phone Numbers Priority 7

Indian (+91, 0091, 0, 10-digit 6–9) and E.164 international numbers. Normalizes Indian variants to one key; rejects dates and ORD- IDs.

Valid: +91 98765 43210, 09876543210 Rejected: 2026-11-27, ORD-9876543210

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does RedactLocal work with zero internet access over file://?

    Yes. All scripts load via classic <script defer> tags with relative paths and zero ES module imports, external fonts, or CDNs, so opening index.html directly from disk works offline.

    Why are Luhn-rejected 16-digit numbers tracked instead of flagged as PII?

    Invalid 16-digit tracking codes or failed test numbers should not be treated as real card numbers—and tracking them as exclusion zones prevents inner digits from accidentally triggering Phone or Aadhaar false positives.

    How does CSV-Aware Mode preserve complex spreadsheets?

    An RFC 4180 parser extracts each cell cleanly while preserving quoted boundaries, escaped "" quotes, embedded commas/newlines, and original line endings, while sharing one Pseudonymization Map across all rows.

    When should I toggle Strict Aadhaar Verhoeff Check off?

    Keep it ON for production data to reject random 12-digit numbers. Turn it OFF only when testing synthetic 12-digit Aadhaar placeholders that were not generated with a valid Verhoeff check digit.

    What is stored in browser localStorage?

    Only your Dark/Light theme choice (redactlocal_theme) and aggregate numeric counters (redactlocal_stats_v1). Your text, files, and pseudonym tables are never written to storage.
